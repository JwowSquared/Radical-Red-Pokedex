What does uploading your save do?

Uploading your save is used with Ability Randomizer and Learnset Randomizer. The general pokemon info will change to match your specific randomizer seed.

Filters

Name: search for a species by name.

Type: Show all species with a certain type.

Move: Show all species who can learn a certain move.

Ability: Show all species who can have a certain ability.

Location: Show all species obtainable at a certain location.

Advanced Search Guide

Build queries with and, or, not, and parentheses.

Comparison: use >, >=, <, <=, =, and != for numbers and exact matches.

Text and list matching: use has for contains, ~ for match-any in a tuple, and !~ for exclude-any matching.

Useful attributes: name, type, ability, moveset, location, locationoriginal, originalpokemon, available, hp, atk, def, spa, spd, spe, bst.

Examples

bst >= 600 and type = dragon

atk <= 95 and ability has 'guard'

location > 'route 10'

locationoriginal <= 'route 5'

ability ~ ('huge power', 'feline prowess')

name !~ ('arceus', 'mewtwo')
